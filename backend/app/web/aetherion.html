Aetherion is Seldrune's experimental software-defined compute fabric: an orchestration layer designed to pool, route and benchmark real accelerators while researching quantum-inspired scheduling and ephemeral workloads. It is not a physical or quantum GPU and makes no claim of exceeding RTX PRO 6000 until benchmarks prove it.
